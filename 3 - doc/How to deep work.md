How to deep work!

1. Fix your env, get into somewhere inspiring. 

2. No distractions during work. NONE. 

3. Short time blocks of work. 
    3.1) Schedule your day in batches of similar tasks 
    3.2) Schedule your deep work as early as possible
    3.3) Schedule buffer and contingency for flexibility

4. Batch up similar tasks. 

5. Do good work early in the day. 

6. Schedule contingency. 

7. Ritualize entering deep work state. 

8. Meditate to be accustomed to silence. 

9. Do something from start to finish in one shot. 

10. Prioritize sleep.  Relax in the evening. But do 
something meaningful and fun. Can study.

11. Write out list for work the next day, before work day ends.
